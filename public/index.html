Settings & Configuration

Meta WhatsApp Webhook Configuration

Meta cannot reach localhost URLs. Configure a public domain or tunnel (ngrok / cloudflare) in PUBLIC_BASE_URL.
Quick Meta Setup Steps:
  1. Log in to Meta Developer Dashboard > Your WhatsApp App > Configuration > Webhook > Edit.
  2. Paste the Callback URL and Verify Token above, then click Verify and save.
  3. Under Webhook fields, click Manage and subscribe to messages.
